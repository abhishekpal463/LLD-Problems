1. Customer can browse a restaurant's menu.
2. Customer can place an order from one restaurant.
3. Customer can make a payment.
4. Restaurant can accept/reject the order.
5. Restaurant prepares the order.
6. A delivery partner is assigned.
7. Delivery partner picks up and delivers the order.
8. Customer can view order status.
